My journey into cybersecurity began with witnessing the growing cyber scam and fraud ecosystems across Southeast Asia. Seeing how these operations exploit technology and impact real people sparked my curiosity about the digital threats and deep-rooted sense of purpose to gurad the digital world.

Coming from a background in building data pipelines, I've developed hands-on experience working with data, solving technical problems, and building practical solutions. Today, I'm channeling that experience into a focused career transition toward cybersecurity, specializing in Digital Forensics and the investigation of cybercrime.

The "Detective" Persona
======
I am deeply committed to understanding the evolving landscape of cyber warfare in the age of AI, with a particular focus on intelligence gathering and Digital Forensics across the legal and financial sectors.

I see cybersecurity as a dynamic contest of intelligence, adaptation, and strategy — an ongoing battle unfolding across the wire. My approach is driven by a curiosity for uncovering weak signals within noisy environments, tracing digital footprints, and connecting fragmented evidence into coherent, meaningful narratives.

Currently Building
======
*   **[Malware Traffic Analysis](/portfolio/malware-traffic-analysis/):** Hands-on network detection work — parsing real C2 pcaps (NetSupport RAT, FormBook) with Python/scapy, then writing statistically robust beacon and fan-out detection scripts, validated against real malware samples. This is the "Detective" persona in practice, and the most concrete piece of my pivot so far.
*   **[Scam Hunter](/portfolio/scam-hunter/):** A defensive toolkit against Southeast-Asia scam patterns — one Python detection core that scores suspicious links (typosquat heuristics, RDAP domain age, URLhaus / Safe Browsing), phone numbers, USDT (TRON) wallets (on-chain Tether blacklist check) and fake "high-salary" job ads (trilingual rules + a TF-IDF classifier), served through a CLI, a Telegram bot and a web page, with a pytest suite. 
*   **Foundations:** preparing for the **CCNA** (networking) and practicing Linux hands-on.
